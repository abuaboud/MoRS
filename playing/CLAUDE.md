# What we've learned about this world

Lessons that apply to every account. Add to this whenever something surprises you, and keep each entry to one or two lines. Per-account notes go in `playing/<account>/CLAUDE.md`.

## The server
- XP is heavily boosted. Fishing went from 27 to 30 in a few casts, so don't plan around normal XP rates.
- Unknown usernames are registered on first login. "Account still logged in" after a crash clears in about 30s.

## Getting around
- "stuck at x,y; a door/gate may be in the way" almost always means a closed door or gate. Look in `objects` for one with `Open` near that tile.
- `walkTo` finds its own way around fences and rivers, but only within the ~50 tiles loaded around you. For longer trips it can walk into a dead end ("no way from x,y …"): walk along the roads instead, with waypoints at most ~40 tiles apart (e.g. Lumbridge → Varrock: north along the road east of the castle, x≈3240, not across the fields).
- "I can't reach that!" means the target is behind a wall or door. `sdk.client.act("collision", {x, y})` shows walls (`0` = free) and helps find the way in.
- The Lumbridge wheat field (around 3162, 3290) can only be entered through its south gate.

## SDK quirks
- `inventory.use` used to fail ("widget not found") because "Use" is an item's target verb, not one of its widget actions. Fixed in `Bridge.findWidget`. If a login says "still logged in" after a client restart, another client container is still logged in as that account (`docker ps`): stop it.
- `inventory.use(item, {object})` sometimes can't find the item. Try the object's own option instead (the windmill hopper has "Fill"), or click the item at its screen position.
- On Tutorial Island, a tab click (Quest List, Equipment) sometimes doesn't register. Click the tab icon directly: Combat 543,186 · Quest 609,186 · Equipment 672,186.
- `inventory.use` and `widgets.click("Wind Strike")` fail on Tutorial Island ("widget not found"). Click the inventory slot (x=578+col*42, y=230+row*36), then `act("object",{name,option:"Use"})`.
- `npc.interact` often says "no menu entry" when trees/walls hide the target and the camera won't move. Walk to an adjacent tile and retry.
- Level-up dialogues can block tutorial progress (ranged rat kill didn't count until they were dismissed). Run `dialogue.continueAll()` after fights.
- Magic tab, top row: 565 = Lumbridge Home Teleport, 613 = Wind Strike. NEVER click spell icons blind: Home Teleport works on Tutorial Island and dumps you in Lumbridge mid-tutorial.
- Tutorial Island in one run, see `playing/islander2`. "no menu entry" / "not found on screen" there is almost always the camera: `camera.set({yaw, pitch:383})` to face the target (0=N, 512=W, 1024=S, 1536=E), then retry. (Before Oct 14, `camera.set` and `turnTo` sent the wrong angle units, so pitch stayed flat and yaw barely turned. That's fixed: `state.camera` now reads back what you set.)
- Tutorial tabs: `openTab("Inventory")` can hit the Combat tab. Click the backpack at 643,186 before slot clicks. Close the bank window (X at 486,23) before you walk off, or every click lands on it.
- Tutorial gates (survival 3089,3091/2, mine 3094,9502, rat pen 3111,9518): "Open" moves you through. You can kill the melee rat from inside the pen, but to leave, open the pen gate from inside, then walk south-east around it to the instructor.
- Tutorial fishing: two of the three shrimp spots say "I can't reach that!". Try each by `act("npc",{index, option:"Net"})`. The smithing menu isn't in `ui`: the Dagger is at screen 42,70. Wind Strike now works via `widgets.click("Wind Strike","Cast")` then `act("npc",{index, option:"Cast"})`. Bank tutorial step jumped 510 → 525 on opening the booth.
- Tutorial last step: `ui` shows the instructor's "go to the mainland?" line first. Press space, then `choose("Yes, take me to the mainland.")`.
- If the game view is zoomed right into a wall and every target is "not found on screen", the zoom slider is maxed. Open Settings → Display tab (703,220) and click the left end of the zoom slider (610,318).
- If every tree shows as "Oak tree", you're looking at the wrong cluster. Walk on and look for "Tree".

## Money
- Fastest first 1k from nothing: pickpocket Man/Woman around Lumbridge castle (3222,3218). Pays 3gp as a "Coin pouch", which stacks: `inventory.interact("Coin pouch","Open-all")` turns them into coins, ~200gp per 5 min. Thieving 1→63 in ~25 min.
- Each failed pickpocket stuns you and costs ~1 HP, so HP runs out, not coins. Bring food: net shrimp/anchovies at Lumbridge swamp (3243,3152, ~6 min to fill), cook on the castle kitchen range (3212,3215).
- Cooking range: `act("object",{name:"Cooking range",option:"Cook"})`, then `key "space"`. `inventory.use(raw, {object})` fails. Once you pick one item, the quantity drops to 1, so loop cook+space for each item.
- walkTo from the swamp to the castle stalls around 3227,3185: call it again and it gets there.
